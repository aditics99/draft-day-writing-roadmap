Sample cover letter

Dear Editor, Thank you for considering my story titled “The Stench of Jasmine.” It is 2,174 words long. This piece explores the juxtaposition of ordinary rituals and grief. I admire [Publication]’s commitment to publishing work that challenges and resonates deeply. I believe this story aligns with your mission of showcasing diverse voices and layered narratives. My bio is: Aditi Chandrasekar is a writer living in Bangalore. Her fiction has appeared in Muse India, Kitaab, The Punch Magazine, Out of Print and The Chakkar. Her work has been shortlisted for the BWW R.K. Anand Prize and the Rama Mehta Writing Grant. She is a contributor in Our Ramayanas by Devdutt Pattanaik. I look forward to hearing from you. Thank you. Regards, Aditi Chandrasekar
